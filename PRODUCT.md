# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local customers in Amman, Jordan (Rainbow Street / شارع قوس قزح area), including students, parents, teachers, and professionals looking for high-quality stationery, school supplies, and office essentials. They visit the site to browse products, learn about the store, and initiate contact via phone or WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

A digital storefront for Abaq Al Yasmina Stationery that showcases product categories, current offers, and contact information. The site bridges the physical store's presence to online discovery — helping customers find what they need and reach out to purchase.

## Brand Personality

Modern, Premium, Approachable

## Anti-references

Generic template-based stationery sites (overly cluttered, poor typography, low-quality imagery). Should avoid looking like a basic e-commerce template — needs to feel curated and boutique.

## Design Principles

- Curated showcase — products are displayed intentionally, not dumped in grids
- Locally grounded — the Rainbow Street location and local community feel are part of the brand
- Clear friction-free contact — the primary action is getting the customer to reach out
- Quality over quantity — fewer products shown well, rather than exhaustive listings

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- RTL-first layout (Arabic language primary)
- WCAG AA contrast compliance
- Support for reduced motion preferences
